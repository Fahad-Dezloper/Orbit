---
title: "One fair price"
description: "Why no single market sets the price."
---

Every market shows a slightly different price for the same token. If each market were measured
from its own price, a pushed market (like the one at YieldBlox) would look perfectly healthy by
its own standard.

Orbit instead measures **every** market from one **reference price**:

1. Find each market's own mid price and how much it holds near that price.
2. Ignore markets with under $100 of depth, and markets with under 5% of the deepest market's
   depth.
3. Take the **upper median** of the remaining prices.

If no market qualifies, the token has no reference and every figure is zero.

<Check>
  A thin market cannot move the reference, and no market vouches for its own price. To shift
  the reference, an attacker would need real money in several markets at once.
</Check>

## Price gaps

When a market's own price is far from the reference, the dashboard data notes it (for example
"own price 14.88% below the reference"). A large gap is a sign that a market is thin or out of
date.
