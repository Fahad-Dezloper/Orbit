---
title: "Safe limit"
description: "Turning depth into a collateral cap a lender can enforce."
---

A liquidator paid a bonus `b` only acts if they can sell the seized collateral at most `b`
below the price. So the exit at band `b` is the most collateral that can be liquidated on
Stellar at that bonus.

```text
liquidatable_now  = exit at the largest sampled band not above the bonus
24h minimum       = lowest liquidatable value over the last 24 hours
recommended cap   = 24h minimum × (1 − safety margin)        default margin: 50%
```

## Example

USTRY with a 5% liquidation bonus:

| Step | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Sellable today before a 5% drop | about $311K |
| Lowest over the day (assume the same) | about $311K |
| Recommended cap with a 50% margin | about $155K |

A lender should accept at most about $155K of USTRY as collateral across all its loans.

<Warning>
  The cap is one input, not a guarantee. Use it with a borrow limit and a rate limit, and treat
  "no figure" as "do not lend".
</Warning>

Read it from [`/v1/assets/{code}/cap`](/api/cap) or compute it on-chain from
[`fresh_depth`](/contract/reading).
