---
title: "Exit liquidity"
description: "Dollars a seller could raise before the price falls too far."
---

**Exit liquidity at a band** is the number of US dollars a seller could raise, across every
Stellar market, before the **average** sale price falls more than that band below the fair
price.

## Bands

| Band | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `200` (2%) | A careful sale, small price impact |
| `1000` (10%) | The headline figure on the dashboard |
| `2500` (25%) | A forced sale |

The published oracle bands are 2%, 10% and 25%. The **liquidation curve** samples twelve bands:
0.5%, 1%, 2%, 3%, 5%, 7.5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 35% and 50%.

<Note>
  Bands use the **average** price of the whole sale, not the last fill. That matches what a
  liquidator actually receives.
</Note>

## How each market is measured

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Order book (SDEX)">
    Walk the bids from the best price down, filling each in turn, until the average price would
    pass the band.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Constant-product pools">
    Aquarius standard pools and Soroswap pairs follow `x · y = k`. The largest sale inside the
    band is solved exactly from the reserves and the pool's fee.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Quoted pools">
    Aquarius concentrated and stable pools, and Sushi v3 pools, use their own maths. Orbit asks
    the pool for quotes at increasing sale sizes and takes the largest sample inside the band.
    It never interpolates between samples, so it can only understate.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Markets are summed in USD. XLM-paired markets are converted with the live XLM/USDC price.

## Verdicts

The dashboard labels each token by its exit at the 10% band:

| Verdict | Exit at 10% |
| --- | --- |
| No buyers | under $100 |
| Very thin | $100 to $10K |
| Thin market | $10K to $100K |
| Deep market | $100K or more |
