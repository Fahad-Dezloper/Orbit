| Market | Type | How Orbit reads it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SDEX | Stellar's built-in order book | Horizon order books against USDC and XLM |
| Aquarius standard pools | Constant product | Reserves, solved exactly |
| Aquarius concentrated and stable pools | Custom curves | The pool's own `estimate_swap` quotes |
| Soroswap | Constant product | Reserves, solved exactly |
| Sushi v3 | Concentrated liquidity | The pool's own `quote_exact_input` quotes |

## Pools are pinned

Orbit does not discover pools automatically. Each pool is listed in `assets.toml` and checked on
every read:

- its tokens must be exactly the measured token and the counter (USDC or XLM);
- its type must match how it is listed;
- what it reports must not exceed what the token contracts say it holds.

A pool that fails any check is left out and noted. Leaving a market out can only make a figure
lower, never higher.

## Why pools matter

For several tokens nearly all of the market is outside the order book:

- **USDY**: about 98% of what can be sold sits in one Aquarius pool.
- **deJAAA and deJTRSY**: the only markets are Sushi v3 pools, about $1.5M each sellable within
  10%.
- **USTRY**: about 30% of its market is in AMM pools.
