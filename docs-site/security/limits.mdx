---
title: "Known limits"
description: "What Orbit does not do yet."
---

<Warning>
  Read these before relying on Orbit's figures. Each one makes a figure lower than reality, or is
  stated openly here.
</Warning>

- **Not audited.** The contracts and publisher have had an internal security review, not an
  external audit. Mainnet comes after one.
- **Markets not read yet:** Curve, Comet and Stellar Broker. Leaving a market out only lowers a
  figure.
- **Pinned pools only.** New pools are not counted until they are added.
- **Quoted pools are sampled.** Exit between samples is not credited. Sushi quotes are sampled up
  to 80% of the pool's USDC.
- **Stellar markets only.** Selling on other chains or redeeming with the issuer is not counted.
- **XLM and USDC themselves** are not measured yet; they are the price units.
- **Order books come from one provider** (Horizon), which is nearing end-of-life.
- **Patient capital** can raise a figure today; see [Threat model](/security/threat-model).
- **Tokens whose issuer file cannot be fetched** are left out on purpose (for example Matrixdock's
  XAUM).
