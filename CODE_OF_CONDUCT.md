# Code of conduct

Orbit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and keep
discussion on topic. Harassment of any kind is not accepted.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository's **Security → Report a
vulnerability** form (it reaches the maintainers only) or to a maintainer directly. Reports are
handled confidentially.
